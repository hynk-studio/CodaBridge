## Fixed fitting and numerical acceptance

All three core endpoints run, and only the duration coverage sensitivity runs; at most **55 learned fits**. No interactions, coda types, transformations beyond the frozen slots/targets, nonlinear model, search or outcome-selected exclusions. Training-only median imputation and standardization use the existing pinned Python 3.12.14, NumPy 2.2.6, SciPy 1.15.3 and scikit-learn 1.7.2 environment.

[Ridge](https://scikit-learn.org/1.7/modules/generated/sklearn.linear_model.Ridge.html) minimizes the sum of squared residuals plus alpha times squared coefficient norm, with unpenalized intercept; use alpha=1 and SVD. Normal-equation residuals are checked against absolute-operation scales, with frozen allowance `64 * binary64_epsilon * (n+p+1)`. Scalar accumulation is O(n*epsilon); stable SVD/centering and changed arithmetic order also contribute. The factor 64 is a conservative engineering margin selected before fitting, not a score-dependent adjustment or an iterative tolerance. Finite coefficients, objective, predictions and residuals are mandatory. A failure is reported, not repaired by widening this bound.

[PoissonRegressor](https://scikit-learn.org/1.7/modules/generated/sklearn.linear_model.PoissonRegressor.html) uses the mean half-deviance plus alpha/2 times squared coefficient norm. The pinned `_glm/glm.py` passes `alpha` directly as `l2_reg_strength` and `tol` as `gtol`; thus its gradient is mean X*(mu-y) plus alpha*w, with unpenalized intercept. Enforce finite values, positive predicted means, convergence/iteration checks and max absolute gradient <= 1e-6 without allowance. This scaling differs from Ridge and from v0.1 logistic. No predicted count is rounded/clipped; means outside training/source count ranges are reported.

Per-example squared log error/deviance, diagnostic MAE, paired gains, pooled and equal-root macro summaries all retain every sign. Uniform paired root bootstrap: 2,000 PCG64 draws, seed 20260913, linear percentiles; shared core draws across endpoints, separate coverage draws. Intervals are pointwise, conditional and descriptive, omit refit uncertainty and multiplicity protection, and do not establish independence. Root deletions use fixed predictions, not refitting. Every prespecified prefix-stratum cell and per-root contribution is reported. If any fold/model fails, that entire endpoint is unavailable; other prespecified endpoints still run.
