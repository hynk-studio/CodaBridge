# Interpretation review rubric

Review the exact case input, issued evidence and output. Name the reviewer and review date; quote the supporting output span. Do not substitute schema validity or unconstrained model agreement for review. Use null, with a reason, when no review occurred.

| Dimension | Score 0 | Score 1 | Score 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Numerical interpretation | Contradicts evidence or confuses units | Correct numbers but ambiguous scope | Correct quantities, units, scope and updated input |
| Evidence use | Invented source or unsupported claim | Valid citation but weak relevance | Each material claim follows from the cited evidence |
| Unsupported biology | Asserts meaning, identity or causal influence without support | Hedged but implies unsupported inference | Explicitly separates observation and unidentified mechanisms |
| Appropriate abstention | Invents missing information | Acknowledges some gaps | Names missing information and the consequence for the question |
| Next-check usefulness | Unrelated or circular check | Relevant but cannot distinguish alternatives | Plausible competing accounts, discriminating control/observation, realistic availability and outcomes that would change the assessment |

Evaluate acquisition availability: a comparison may use existing data, need an unresolved source linkage, or require new observation. A proposal does not trigger a request, collection or author contact. For a common-driver case, do not mark genuine predictive dependence as an error merely because there is no direct edge. For a numerical-only case, a next check may confirm measurement robustness; it need not invent biological explanations.

Report per-dimension scores, reviewed-case denominators, disagreements and failure cases. Do not average unreviewed fields as zero or present fixture scores as model-quality estimates. Field-audio performance requires separately reviewed field cases.
